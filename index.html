IoT Smart Pollution & Safety Monitoring System

With Automatic Ventilation and Alcohol-Interlock Control

VIT Chennai — Electronics & Communication Engineering

About the Project

An IoT-based smart system that continuously monitors air quality, temperature, humidity, and alcohol levels in real time. When unsafe conditions are detected, the system automatically activates a ventilation fan, triggers a buzzer alert, and engages an ignition lock to prevent drunk driving.

How It Works

1

Sensors continuously collect gas levels, temperature, humidity, and alcohol data

2

ESP8266 processes the data and compares with predefined safety thresholds

3

If unsafe: ventilation fan activates, buzzer sounds, ignition lock engages

4

Real-time data and alerts are sent to Flask server via WiFi

5

Mobile app receives live notifications and displays sensor readings
